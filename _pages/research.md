# Research
Updating soon -- stay put. (April 27 2026)

The BAEG research group uses fruit flies as a model to conduct research on diabetes, obesity, muscular atrophy, aging, and stem cell development. The group employs a variety of methods, including animal disease models, histochemistry, molecular biology, cell biology, and deep sequencing.
